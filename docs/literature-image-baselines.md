## Points that matter for our comparison
- Everyone uses pooled pixel-level metrics (AP or AUC-PR, F1, IoU) over a window around the fire; we score per case (direction AUC, IoU at the true area, hits in the top 200) against nearest-first. Our pooled AP, F1 and IoU have to be computed before any number is put next to theirs.
- Their persistence / previous-mask baseline is the counterpart of our nearest-first rule: AUC-PR 0.115 against 0.284 for the best model in Huot et al.
- Absolute AP depends on how many cells burn (prevalence), the resolution and the region, so published values from the US are not a target to beat; they are a reference for scale (0.28 to 0.48).
- The FireSenseNet paper shows two ways scores get inflated (predicting both days so the previous mask is reproduced; turning unknown labels into "no fire"); the metric must be defined strictly. WildfireSpreadBench warns that AP alone can rank models wrongly.
- Common image baselines we do not have yet: Res18-U-Net, CNN autoencoder in the style of Huot, SegFormer / Swin-UNet, and time-series models (ConvLSTM, UTAE). We have Res50-U-Net and FireSenseNet.
